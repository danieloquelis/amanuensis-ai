# Sound credits

Instrument samples served by Amanuensis from this folder. `manifest.json` lists every file with its source;
`yarn sounds:fetch` downloads them (they are not kept in git).

- **Splendid Grand Piano** (`splendid-grand-piano`): AKAI Professional, via sfzinstruments and smpldsnds. License: Public domain. https://github.com/smpldsnds/sfzinstruments-splendid-grand-piano
- **FluidR3 GM (guitar, bass, trumpet, flute, trombone)** (`fluidr3-gm`): Frank Wen (Fluid R3); converted by Benjamin Gleitzman (midi-js-soundfonts). License: CC BY 3.0 as published by midi-js-soundfonts; Fluid R3 itself is MIT. https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
- **Versilian Community Sample Library (drum kit)** (`vcsl`): Versilian Studios (Samuel Gossner); ogg/m4a conversion by smpldsnds. License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
